# Solitaire Scoundrel — Support

Welcome to the support page for **Solitaire Scoundrel**, a classic card-based game.

## Need help?

If you have questions, encounter a bug, or have feedback about the game, please contact us by email.

**Support email:** robertandersonj@icloud.com

When reporting a bug, please include:
- A description of what happened
- The steps needed to reproduce the issue, if possible
- Your device model and operating system version

Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in your support request.

## About the Game

Solitaire Scoundrel is a single-player card game where you battle cards, manage your health, use weapon and potion cards, and try to achieve your best score.
